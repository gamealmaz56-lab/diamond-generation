Self-diagnosis

Check yourself
5 signs of resource loss

  • 01

    Paralysis of strategic decisions

    Important questions lie unresolved for weeks. Not because there is no information — because the brain can't handle it. You are drowning in operations, and the strategy is standing still.

  • 02

    Waking up in the middle of the night — unable to stop the mind

    3–4 AM. The brain spontaneously returns to work scenarios — as if continuing a shift you didn't declare. It's impossible to turn it off by willpower.

  • 03

    Achieving a goal — and feeling nothing

    Closed a deal, hit the quarterly target — and instead of joy, there's deafening fatigue and emptiness. Winning hurts no less than losing. It's not laziness — it's a system signal.

  • 04

    You are at home, but you are not there

    You speak with your family briefly, to the point — so as not to waste resources. Conversations have become like work tasks. Your loved ones feel it. You do too, but you can't do anything.

  • 05

    Your head doesn't work like it used to

    You read an email three times — and still can't formulate a response. Simple tasks take twice as long. You explain it as a "bad day," but there are fewer and fewer good ones.

If these situations describe your reality — you are not just tired. You are at the limit. The safety margin does not deplete gradually. It runs out in one day. The "4 Pillars" methodology was created exactly to overcome this state.

Methodology

Four Pillars

01

Mental Pillar

Ability to think clearly

Your ability to make quick and clear decisions. When this pillar sags, you begin to doubt, spend hours playing the same scenarios in your head, and waste a lot of energy on simple tasks. Because of mental overload, the business begins to stall.

02

Physical Pillar

Your energy level

The state of your body. Chronic fatigue and lack of sleep are not just a "weakness of character," but physiology that blocks the brain's work. Without a strong and rested body, it is impossible to withstand high loads in business.

03

Spiritual Pillar

Understanding true goals

The answer to the question "Why am I doing all this?". If this pillar is destroyed, even new profit records stop pleasing you. Work turns into a routine by inertia, which very quickly leads to complete burnout.

04

Social Pillar

Your environment and team

The ability of the business to work without your constant intervention. If all processes are locked on you, you cannot relax even at home with your family. A strong team is the key to your personal freedom.
